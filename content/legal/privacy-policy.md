## 1. Information We Collect
We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you subscribe to our newsletters, sign up for waitlists, or apply for open roles. This may include your name, email address, CV/resume, and any message details you provide.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information to:
- Deliver newsletter updates and announcements regarding our research labs and protocols.
- Evaluate employment applications for our open research and systems engineering roles.
- Respond to inquiries, feedback, or support requests.
- Improve and maintain our websites, tools, and developer platforms.

## 3. Cookies and Analytics
We use necessary cookies to remember layout choices and theme preferences (light/dark mode). We may also gather basic aggregated analytics data to evaluate page loading speeds and overall user experience.

## 4. Information Sharing and Disclosure
Sashvat Bharat does not sell or lease your personal data. We only share information with reliable service providers who assist us in managing email broadcasts or processing employment applications, strictly subject to confidentiality terms.

## 5. Data Security
We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and digital safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or manipulation.
